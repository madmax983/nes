⚡ Bolt: Pre-allocate vectors in core emulator

💡 What: Changed `Vec::new()` to `Vec::with_capacity(64)` for temporary buffers (`scratch_writes`, `last_cpu_bus_trace`, `mmio_reads`, etc.) in `NesCore::new` and `Cpu::new`.
🎯 Why: These vectors are used heavily during CPU stepping in a clear-and-swap pattern. Not pre-allocating them causes multiple sequential heap allocations per vector during the first few frames until they reach their stable capacity.
📊 Impact: Removes multiple hidden heap allocations on startup for every new emulator instance, preventing micro-stutters.
🔬 Measurement: Run `cargo bench` or profile the execution graph.

Tested by running `cargo test --all-features`.
