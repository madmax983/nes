## 2025-07-10 - PPU update queue pop unwrap panic risk
**Learning:** `unwrap()` is a common vulnerability pattern for collections popped without a strict upper bound length check or guarantee. Previously, `ppu.rs` used a peek-and-pop method utilizing `.is_some_and(...)` which bounded the check, but ultimately still used `.unwrap()`. Even though mathematically safe from empty queue due to the `is_some_and`, Sentry's principle enforces no `.unwrap()` logic traps to prevent human error during refactoring that might sever the link between the `if/while` check and the `unwrap`.
**Action:** Always prefer combined `match` or `if let Some(x)` bindings to extract queue items securely and branch behavior explicitly, decoupling implicit guarantees.
