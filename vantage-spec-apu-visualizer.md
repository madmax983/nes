# 🔭 Vantage: Spec for APU Visualizer

## 👤 User Story
"As a Homebrew Developer or Musician, I want a real-time APU visualizer and channel mixer, so that I can inspect the active volume, frequency, and duty cycle of each audio channel (Pulse 1, Pulse 2, Triangle, Noise, DPCM) as they are generated by the emulator."

## ❓ So What? (Business Problem)
**What business problem does this solve?**
While we have a CPU debugger for logic and a PPU visualizer for graphics, audio development on the NES is notoriously opaque. Developers and chip-tune artists struggle to balance channel volumes or debug rogue pitch sweeps because the final audio is just a single mixed waveform. By providing a dedicated APU visualizer and mixer, we close the loop on a complete homebrew workstation, attracting the vibrant NES music community (e.g., FamiTracker users) and making our emulator the definitive tool for full-stack NES development.

## 📊 Success Metrics
- **Performance:** Activating the APU visualizer window maintains a steady 60fps and introduces no audio crackling/underruns.
- **Utility:** Developers can easily mute individual channels and see real-time volume envelopes for each of the 5 base channels.
- **Adoption:** 30% of users who load homebrew ROMs open the APU visualizer at least once per session.

## 🕵️ Gap Analysis
- **Market View:** Other development-focused emulators (like Mesen) feature robust APU tools, including piano rolls and individual channel mute toggles.
- **Our Gap:** We currently mix all audio channels internally in `nes-core` and output a single PCM stream. We do not expose the internal APU channel state visually or allow interactive mixing, making audio debugging a frustrating, trial-and-error process.

## ✅ Acceptance Criteria
- Must provide a separate UI window or overlay tab to view real-time APU state.
- Must display the 5 standard APU channels (Pulse 1, Pulse 2, Triangle, Noise, DPCM) with real-time volume meters and current frequency/pitch data.
- Must allow muting/unmuting of individual channels to isolate audio.
- Must update visually in real-time as the emulator runs.
- Must reflect the exact APU state when emulation is paused.

## 🚫 Out of Scope
- Piano roll timeline visualization (Phase 2).
- Expansion audio channels (VRC6, MMC5, etc.) (Phase 2).
- Direct manipulation of APU registers via the visualizer (read-only inspection for Phase 1, except for muting).
